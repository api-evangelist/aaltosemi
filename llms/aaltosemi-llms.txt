# AaltoSemi

> AaltoSemi (芯爱科技（南京）有限公司) is an integrated-circuit packaging substrate
> manufacturer in Nanjing, China. It fabricates the organic substrates that carry the
> electrical connection between a semiconductor die and a printed circuit board.
> AaltoSemi publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable contract.

## About

- Founded: 8 May 2021
- Headquarters: Pukou Economic Development Zone, Nanjing, Jiangsu, China
- Sector: Semiconductors — advanced packaging / IC substrates
- Products: Coreless, ETS, FCCSP, FCBGA (BT), FCBGA (ABF) substrates, in both BT and ABF
  material systems, offered as an end-to-end R&D, design, production and test service
- Markets served: consumer electronics, high-performance computing, communications,
  automotive, medical
- Quality certifications: IATF 16949, VDA 6.3
